### I think we can all agree the perfect sandwhich is the B.L.T *(Bacon, Lettuce, Tomatoes)* Here we'll go through the steps of making the perfect B.L.T....Let's begin.


#### What you'll need:
- *``` Bread```*
- *```Tomatoes```*
- *```Lettuce```*
- *```Bacon```*
- *```Mayonnaise```*

##### Next let's discus the steps to making the perfect B.L.T

> Fist we want to make sure our bread is lightly toasted. 
>> Next we want to bake or grill our bacon to a desired taste.
>>>Then we want to slice our tomatoes and lettuce.
>>>>After that we want want to spread our mayonnaise evenly across the toasted bread.
>>>>>Finally we put it all together.
